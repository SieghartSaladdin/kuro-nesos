# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Kuro Nesos to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality,
appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone
represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer, [@SieghartSaladdin](https://github.com/SieghartSaladdin),
through GitHub. Every report is reviewed and handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone
whose behaviour is harmful.
